Evaluation and Reporting\n\n # Context:\n ## Domain Name: Mimris Modeling App\n\n## Domain Definition:\nThe Mimris Modeling App is a versatile and comprehensive modeling tool designed for cyclic modeling and metamodeling. It leverages graphical symbols and type views to represent various model views of the same model objects and relationships. This tool supports the storage of models locally as JSON files and on GitHub, promoting collaboration and version control.\n\n### Domain Scope:\nThe Mimris Modeling App encompasses several key areas within the broader field of modeling:\n1. **Enterprise Modeling**: Creating models that represent the structure, processes, and operations of an entire organization.\n2. **Process Modeling**: Designing and analyzing business processes to improve efficiency and effectiveness.\n3. **Workspace Modeling**: Modeling the physical and digital workspaces to optimize workflows and resource utilization.\n4. **POPS (Product-Organization-Process-Service) Modeling**: Integrating models of products, organizations, processes, and services to provide a holistic view.\n5. **IRTV (Information-Role-Task-View) Modeling**: Modeling the relationships between information, roles, tasks, and views to support decision-making and communication.\n6. **AKM (Active Knowledge Modeling)**: Modeling knowledge and expertise within an organization to facilitate knowledge sharing and innovation.\n\n### Key Domain Concepts and Terms:\n1. **Cyclic Modeling**: The iterative process of creating, refining, and updating models.\n2. **Metamodeling**: The process of creating models that define the structure and semantics of other models.\n3. **Graphical Symbols**: Visual representations used to depict model objects and relationships.\n4. **Type Views**: Different perspectives or views of the same model objects to cater to various stakeholders.\n5. **JSON Files**: A lightweight data interchange format used for storing models locally.\n6. **GitHub**: A web-based platform for version control and collaboration, used for storing and sharing models.\n7. **Stakeholders**: Individuals or groups with an interest in or influence over the modeling process.\n8. **Version Control**: The management of changes to documents, programs, and other information stored as computer files.\n\n### Primary Objectives:\n1. **Model Creation and Management**: Enable users to create, edit, and manage models efficiently.\n2. **Collaboration**: Facilitate collaboration among stakeholders through shared model repositories.\n3. **Version Control**: Ensure that changes to models are tracked and managed effectively.\n4. **Stakeholder Identification**: Identify and categorize stakeholders by their roles or involvement in the modeling process.\n5. **Knowledge Sharing**: Promote the sharing of knowledge and expertise within the organization.\n6. **Process Improvement**: Use models to analyze and improve business processes.\n\n### Current Limitations and Boundaries:\n1. **Technical Constraints**: Dependence on JSON format for local storage may limit compatibility with other systems.\n2. **Organizational Constraints**: Requires buy-in from stakeholders and may face resistance to change.\n3. **Financial Constraints**: Initial investment in the tool and training for users.\n4. **Regulatory Constraints**: Compliance with data protection and privacy regulations when storing models on GitHub.\n5. **Operational Constraints**: Ensuring that the tool integrates seamlessly with
